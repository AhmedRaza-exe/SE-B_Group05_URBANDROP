How Fit Preference Adjusts Your Cut

  • Slim: Steps down one size when borderline for a cleaner torso line.
  • Regular: Matches our intended boxy streetwear proportion.
  • Oversized: Steps up one size for maximum sleeve stack and dropped-shoulder volume.
