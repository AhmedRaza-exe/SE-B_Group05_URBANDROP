01 // Shipping

DISPATCH & DELIVERY
All standard orders placed before 3:00 PM PKT are packed and dispatched the same business day. Delivery takes 1–2 business days within Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi, and 3–5 business days for all other cities nationwide. During major seasonal drops, please allow an extra 24 hours for quality control and serial numbering.
Standard tracked courier shipping is a flat rate of PKR 250 across Pakistan. Orders above PKR 10,000 automatically qualify for free express shipping. Cash on Delivery (COD) and prepaid card/wallet orders carry no hidden handling fees.
Yes. Same-day In-Store Pickup is available at our Islamabad (F-7 & Centaurus), Lahore (Gulberg & DHA Phase 5), and Karachi (Clifton & DHA Phase 6) flagships. Bring your digital order confirmation and valid photo ID within 5 days of receiving the ready-for-pickup SMS.

02 // Returns

EXCHANGES & REFUNDS
You may request a return or size exchange within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, free of cologne or marks, and returned with all original URBANDROPS hangtags, zip-locks, and dust bags intact. Size exchanges can also be completed immediately at any flagship store.
Numbered archive drops and collaboration capsules are eligible for size exchanges or store credit only (while replacement stock lasts), unless the garment arrived defective or damaged. Standard core collection pieces qualify for full refunds back to your original payment method or bank transfer for COD orders.
Once your return parcel arrives at our fulfillment hub, quality inspection takes up to 48 hours. Approved refunds are issued within 3–5 business days to your bank account, JazzCash/Easypaisa wallet, or original card, and you will receive an email confirmation receipt.

03 // Sizing

FIT & MEASUREMENTS
Most URBANDROPS heavyweight tees (280 GSM) and French terry hoodies (480 GSM) are cut in a boxy, dropped-shoulder streetwear silhouette. Take your usual size for the intended relaxed drape, or size down one step if you prefer a closer standard fit.

Reference flat-lay garment measurements (in inches, ±0.5" tolerance):

Size Chest Width Body Length Shoulder Drop
S 22.5" 27.5" 21.5"
M 24.0" 28.5" 22.5"
L 25.5" 29.5" 23.5"
XL 27.0" 30.5" 24.5"
All URBANDROPS 100% combed cotton jerseys and fleeces are enzyme pre-shrunk during production. To preserve garment dimensions and puff-print graphics, wash inside out on a cold gentle cycle (30°C max) and hang dry flat—never tumble dry on high heat.
